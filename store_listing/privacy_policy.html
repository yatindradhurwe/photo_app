Privacy Policy for PhotoApp

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how YJTechnoSoft ("we", "our", or "us") handles your data and privacy when you use our mobile application PhotoApp (the "App").

1. Information We Access and How We Use It

A. Photos and Videos (Device Storage)

To provide core gallery and album management functionalities, PhotoApp requests read access to your device storage (such as READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, or READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE). This data is processed locally on your device to display your photos and videos and organize them by folder.

We do not transmit, copy, or monitor your media on any private servers.

B. Google Account & Google Drive Access

PhotoApp offers an optional cloud backup integration with Google Drive. If you choose to connect your Google account:

  • We access your Google account profile information (display name, email address, avatar photo) strictly for displaying your logged-in session inside the app.
  • We request the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope, which only permits the app to upload and manage files that PhotoApp itself has created in your personal Google Drive.
  • We cannot view, edit, or delete other unrelated files on your Google Drive.

2. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any personal information or media files with third parties. All communications with Google services occur directly between your device and Google's authenticated APIs.

3. Data Security

All network communications (including Google authentication and file uploads) are encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).

4. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. We encourage you to review this page periodically for any updates.
