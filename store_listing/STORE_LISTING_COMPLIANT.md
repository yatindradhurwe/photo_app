### Full Description — *Max 4,000 characters*
```text
PhotoApp by YJTechnoSoft is an on-device gallery utility designed to help you view, browse, and organize photos and videos stored across your Android device.

Whether managing photos captured by your camera, screenshots, or videos saved from messaging applications, PhotoApp provides an organized interface with timeline navigation and directory-based album browsing.

CORE FEATURES:

1. Unified Media Timeline
• View all stored images and videos in a chronological grid.
• Filter your view between All Media, Photos Only, or Videos Only with one tap.
• Quick scrolling with thumbnail caching for smooth navigation.

2. Folder & Album Organization
• Browse media automatically grouped by device folders (such as Camera, Screenshots, Downloads, and WhatsApp Media).
• Folder cards display album cover previews and current item counts.
• Open any folder to view its contents in a dedicated grid view.

3. Photo Viewer
• Tap any photo for full-screen viewing.
• Pinch-to-zoom and double-tap gestures to inspect image details.
• View photo metadata including resolution, dimensions, file name, and capture date.

4. Built-in Video Player
• Native video playback directly inside the app for recorded and saved video files.
• Interactive playback scrubber to fast-forward, rewind, and check video duration.
• Video duration badges displayed directly on thumbnails in the gallery grid.

5. Multi-Select & Sharing
• Long-press any item to activate multi-selection mode.
• Select multiple photos and videos at once to share via installed apps (such as messaging, email, or social apps).
• Clear selections or select all with a single tap.

6. Optional Google Drive Backup
• Sign in with your Google account to back up selected photos and videos to your personal Google Drive storage.
• Automatically stores uploaded files in a dedicated app folder on your Google Drive.
• View upload progress indicators while transferring files.

PERMISSIONS & DATA PRIVACY:
• Storage / Media Access (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO): Required strictly to discover, index, and display local photos and videos in the gallery and folder views.
• Internet Access: Used exclusively when you choose to connect your Google account and upload selected media to your Google Drive.
• Media files remain stored locally on your device unless you choose to share or back them up.

PhotoApp is developed and maintained by YJTechnoSoft.
Support & Feedback: contact@yjtechnosoft.com
```
